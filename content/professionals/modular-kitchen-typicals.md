# Turriva — Modular kitchen typical details

## Module logic
- Base and wall modules sized from approved shop drawings — not catalogue-only swaps
- Wet zones: moisture-resistant carcass specification for Gulf climates
- Hardware: soft-close, heat-rated zones at cook lines per approved schedule

## Coordination
1. Approved design intent / shop drawings issued
2. Factory fabrication with QC sign-off
3. Phased delivery matched to site access
4. Install, align, snagging, handover

## Typical programme inputs needed
- Floor plan with dimensions
- MEP coordination points (hood, plumbing, power)
- Finish schedule or sample approvals
